Welcome to Dashboard Engine

Your desktop is now a live dashboard — it renders straight onto the wallpaper, behind your icons. Here's what you can do from here.

  • Browse & switch packs. Eight built-in designs are installed. Pick one in Installed, then “Use on desktop”. Find more under Browse.
  • Make it yours. Select a pack and use Customize to recolour it, change the particles, and more — or open it in the editor to rebuild the layout.
  • Wire in your life. The Planner puts events on the wallpaper, Launcher pins your apps, and Assistant connects a local or hosted AI you can talk to.
  • It stays out of the way. The engine lives in your tray and pauses the animation for full-screen apps. Close this window anytime — the wallpaper keeps running.
