Upcoming

Pins appear on any pack with a launcher component (drop one in via the editor). Your pins and recents are personal — they never leave this PC or enter a pack.

The assistant connects to any OpenAI-compatible endpoint you choose — pick one below. Free options exist: a local model (Ollama / LM Studio — no key, fully offline), or a free-tier key from OpenRouter or Groq. Or use your own OpenAI key. A key you enter is optional (local servers need none), stored encrypted on this PC, and never included in a pack or export.

Pick the voice that matches your persona's language — a Chinese persona spoken by an English voice comes out garbled. Presets for each language are listed here, and picking a language persona above auto-selects a matching one. To switch between a female and a male voice, pick that language's preset — male voices are available for English, Spanish and French (the “· Male” presets); Chinese, Japanese and Korean are female-only for now. Your own Voice tuning profiles appear here too. “Default voice” is English and does not follow your tuning.

How many recent messages are sent to the model each turn. A lower number keeps small or local models fast and within their context limit; raise it if your model has a large context window. The full chat is always kept on disk.

Pick a preset above to fill this in, then tweak it — or write your own and save it below. Replies are read aloud, so keep it plain and short — no lists or emoji. Give it a name and a personality, and say how it should address you (for example, by your name). Another language? Choose a language preset (or write your persona in any language and tell it to reply in that language), then pick a matching voice above — the bank includes English, Spanish, French, Chinese, Japanese and Korean.

Language

Choose the interface language. Community translations added to the locales folder appear here automatically. Untranslated text falls back to English.

Display

Choose which monitor shows the animated wallpaper. It moves right away.

Startup

The engine launches quietly to the tray when you sign in, so your desktop is already alive — no window to open.

Voice alerts

Your assistant voice announces when CPU, memory or disk run high, or the battery gets low. Needs a “System info” component with Live health alerts on, and speaks with the voice from Assistant → Voice.

Performance

The animated wallpaper pauses and throttles like Wallpaper Engine so it stays a good background citizen. These are the same controls as the tray’s Performance menu.

Background motion

Scales the parallax and depth drift on layered pack backgrounds — turn it down, or off, if you find the movement distracting. It doesn’t affect performance.

Weather

Background music

Play your own music on the desktop. Files stay on your PC and are never saved into a pack or shared — the wallpaper just plays them, looping through the list.

Help

If something goes wrong, the logs folder holds a diagnostic log (and any crash dumps) you can share when reporting a problem. It contains no personal data.

Welcome to Dashboard Engine

      
Your desktop is now a live dashboard — it renders straight onto the wallpaper,
        behind your icons. Here's what you can do from here.

      
    
        
  • Browse & switch packs. Eight built-in designs are installed. Pick one in
          Installed, then “Use on desktop”. Find more under Browse.
    • 
        
  • Make it yours. Select a pack and use Customize to recolour it, change the
          particles, and more — or open it in the editor to rebuild the layout.
    • 
        
  • Wire in your life. The Planner puts events on the wallpaper, Launcher
          pins your apps, and Assistant connects a local or hosted AI you can talk to.
    • 
        
  • It stays out of the way. The engine lives in your tray and pauses the animation for
          full-screen apps. Close this window anytime — the wallpaper keeps running.
