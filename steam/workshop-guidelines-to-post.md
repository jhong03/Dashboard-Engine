## Dashboard Engine Workshop — Community Content Guidelines

Dashboard Engine lets you share two kinds of creations through the Steam Workshop:
**Dashboard packs** (a skin/layout/persona for the live desktop dashboard) and **Voice
profiles** (tuning settings that restyle one of the app's built-in voices — never an
audio recording).

By publishing to the Workshop you agree to follow these rules. Content that breaks them
will be removed and blocked from installing.

**Do NOT publish content that:**
- Depicts, promotes, or links to sexual content involving minors, or any other illegal content.
- Contains pornography, sexually explicit imagery, or gratuitous/extreme violence or gore.
- Promotes hate, harassment, or discrimination against people based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or similar.
- Reveals a real person's private information, or impersonates a real person or organization.
- Infringes anyone's copyright or trademark — including art, logos, fonts, characters, music, or a third party's voice you don't have the right to use.
- Contains malware, or code that tries to break out of the app's sandbox, access your files/network, or harm your system.
- Is otherwise unlawful or violates the Steam Subscriber Agreement and Steam's Rules & Guidelines.

**Voice profiles** may only reference the app's own licensed built-in voices. The format
stores tuning numbers only — **uploading a voice recording is not possible**, by design.

**Reporting:** every item has a **Report** button in the app (which opens the item's Steam
page, where Steam's own Report control lives) and you can report any item directly on Steam.
You can also email the developer at **dashboardengine.support@gmail.com**.

**Enforcement:** reports are reviewed within **72 hours**. Confirmed violations are added
to a blocklist that stops the item installing for everyone in the next update, and we
request that Steam remove it. Illegal content is escalated to Steam and the appropriate
authorities immediately.
